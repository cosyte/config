The internal-refs canonical rules take what generalizes from dicom's own gate. An umbrella spec id (`S0228-github-profile-ci-drift`, never a HCPCS code such as `S9083`) is an identifier. The phase rule also catches the adjective forms (`in subsequent phases`, `a later wave`, `waves 2 and 3`) while letting the clinical senses through (`a later phase of the disease`), and a citation of the roadmap (`tracked by the roadmap`). The ADR rule reads the plural, `#` and two-digit forms (`ADRs 0015`, `ADR #0018`, `ADR 23`). The jargon rule catches process commentary (`a later PR`, `this changeset`, `out of scope for this change`) but not `this spec`, `this session` or `this port`, which are reader words in a parser, and lets an image slice in its volume through. The repo-path rule catches the planning files a session writes (`CONTEXT.md`, `REQUIREMENTS.md`, `PITFALLS.md`), the meta-repo or umbrella repo named as a place, and its `work/` and `cards/` directories, and a `decisions/NNNN-` path wherever it sits. Measured on the pinned libraries, this adds 12 hits, all of them real (in hl7, x12 and ncpdp), and removes none. dicom's decision-number, issue-number, `WORD-N` and bare section rules stay dicom's: each collides with reference material another library publishes.
