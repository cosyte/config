`internal-refs`: the phase or wave rule now reads the word after `phase` to decide whether it names a phase. A number, a number word (zero to nineteen, the tens to ninety with or without a hyphenated unit such as `twenty-one`, and `hundred`), a letter, a roman numeral, any of the 24 Greek letter names or a capitalized word still names one, so `phase 2`, `phase one`, `phase thirteen`, `phase omega`, `Phase W`, `Phase Atlas`, `phase-by-phase`, `in a later phase` and `wave 3` are still hits. A run of spaces, a tab or a no-break space between `phase` and its name now reads like one space, so `phase  2` is a hit too. An ordinary lowercase word now makes `phase` a noun and passes, so clinical study phase wording in vendored standard data no longer fails the gate: HL7 table 0003's `CRM - Correct/update phase information` and `MFN/MFK - Clinical study without phases but with schedules master file`, and imaging terms such as `phase encoding`. What no longer refuses: `phase` or `Phase` before an ordinary lowercase word, such as `phase foundations` or a sentence that starts `Phase contrast`; a roadmap name written with a capital, a number, a number word or a Greek letter name is still caught. Callers change nothing.
