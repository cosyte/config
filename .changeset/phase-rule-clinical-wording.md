`internal-refs`: the phase or wave rule lets clinical study phase wording through, so vendored standard data such as HL7 table 0003's `CRM - Correct/update phase information` and `MFN/MFK - Clinical study without phases but with schedules master file` no longer fails the gate. The rule's patterns are unchanged and still name every candidate; one narrow excuse now lets a candidate through when it is `phase` or `phases`, a single space, and a word made only of lowercase ASCII letters that ends there and is not a number word (zero to nineteen, the tens, `hundred`), a written ordinal (`first` to `twentieth`), a Greek letter name, a roman numeral made of `i v x l c d m` with at most one letter after it, or a single letter. So `phase information`, `phases but`, `phase encoding` and `Phase contrast` pass, while `phase 2`, `phase one`, `phase iib`, `phase xxiii`, `phase beta`, `phase second`, `phase b`, `Phase Atlas`, `pHaSe Atlas`, `phase-by-phase`, `phase twenty-one`, `in a later phase` and `wave 3` are still hits. Nothing the rule passed before is refused now. Three ordinary words made only of roman numeral letters stay refused on purpose, as they were before: `phase via`, `phase mix` and `phase did`; reword such a sentence. A compiled rule may now carry an `excuse` (only the phase rule does; a caller's extra rule never does), and the new `ruleHits(rule, text)` export reads a rule the way the gate does. Callers change nothing; code that runs a compiled rule's `patterns` itself should call `ruleHits` instead to see what the gate reports.
