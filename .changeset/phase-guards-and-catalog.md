The internal-refs phase rule lets two kinds of ordinary prose through. `phase` as the second half of a compound word is no longer a hit (`connect-phase socket`, `two-phase state`, `read-phase timeout`), and neither is `phase` or `phases` followed by `where`, `are`, `was`, `were`, `during` or `at` (`All 5 phases are defined`, `Connection lifecycle phase where the error occurred`). A phase named beside those words is still a hit (`shipped in phase 2`, `Phase 3 was where it failed`, `Phase-L`), and both guards' passages are in the rule's self-test floor, so every run proves them. The catalog is regenerated from each library's current package.json: the terminology, deid and synth descriptions take the serial comma, the cli record carries cli's current description, and cli's engines are `>=22.0.0`.
